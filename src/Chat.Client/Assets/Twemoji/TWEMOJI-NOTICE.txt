LAB2 Chat Client - bundled Twemoji graphics

The colored emoji PNGs in this folder are from the official Twemoji project:
https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji

Twemoji graphics are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International license (CC BY 4.0). The complete license text is included in
LICENSE-GRAPHICS in this folder. Project source and asset documentation:
https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji/blob/master/LICENSE-GRAPHICS

The client embeds these PNGs locally; it does not load emoji images from a
network URL at runtime. Emoji characters remain Unicode text on the wire.
